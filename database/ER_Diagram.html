Smart College Bus Allocation System

Entity-Relationship Diagram & Database Architectural Model (CollegeBusDB)

13 Relational Tables • InnoDB Engine • utf8mb4 • Referential Integrity Enforced

Architectural Relational Mappings

users (1) — (1) students: One-to-One linked by user_id FK (UNIQUE)
users (1) — (1) teachers: One-to-One linked by user_id FK (UNIQUE)
users (1) — (N) bookings: User can hold multiple trip bookings
buses (1) — (N) trips: A physical vehicle is assigned to multiple trips
routes (1) — (N) trips: A fixed route is scheduled across daily time slots
drivers (1) — (N) trips: A certified driver operates allocated trips
routes (1) — (N) route_stops — (1) bus_stops: Many-to-Many resolved by junction table with ordered sequence
trips (1) — (N) bookings: Passengers reserve seats on a specific trip
trips (1) — (N) seat_requests: Overflow passengers submit requests for admin review
exam_timetable → trips: Recommendation engine pairs exam_end_time with upcoming departure trips
